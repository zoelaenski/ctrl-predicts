From question to payout in four steps

1

Read the entry

The agent writes a short summary with sources for every question, so you know what you are betting on.

2

Bet YES or NO

Connect a NEAR wallet and stake testnet NEAR. The two pools set the price.

3

The agent resolves it

After the market closes, the agent checks the evidence and settles it on-chain.

4

Claim your winnings

Winners share the pool minus a 2% fee. A market with bets on one side only is refunded.

Every answer comes with its evidence

The agent never just says who won. It saves its reasoning and sources, and stores a fingerprint of them on-chain.

  • Prices are settled by a rule, not an opinion

    For crypto markets the agent compares CoinGecko's price at the close time with the line in the question.

  • The AI can't invent sources

    For news questions the model only picks from links the agent fetched. It never writes a URL itself.

  • If it's unclear, it doesn't guess

    When the evidence doesn't settle the question, the market stays unresolved instead of paying out on a hunch.

  • Anyone can check the fingerprint

    A SHA-256 hash of the full resolution record is stored with the market on NEAR.

Questions people ask

Is this real money?

No. Ctrl Predicts runs on NEAR testnet, so you bet with free testnet NEAR. Mainnet is planned, and before real money is involved we want a challenge window on every resolution and a legal review.

Who settles the markets?

An AI agent with its own NEAR account. Only that account can resolve a market, and only after the market has closed.

How do I check an answer?

Open a resolved market. You see the outcome, the agent's reasoning and sources, and the evidence hash that is stored on-chain with the market.

What happens if the evidence is unclear?

The agent does not guess. The market stays unresolved. If everyone bet on one side, the market is refunded.

How does the agent earn?

A 2% fee is taken from the pool at resolution. 30% of it is paid to the resolver agent's account straight away, and 70% goes to the treasury.

Which questions can I bet on?

Crypto prices, music and football today. Crypto prices are settled by a fixed rule on CoinGecko data. Other questions are settled from news sources.
